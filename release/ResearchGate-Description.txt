Object-Centric Structural Reasoning and Evolution (OCSRE) — v1.0.0: Research Framework and Contracts

Object-Centric Structural Reasoning and Evolution (OCSRE) proposes a research framework for connecting object representation, computation, reasoning, in-situ learning, and constrained evolution through typed, auditable object interfaces. It investigates identity-aware and perspective-dependent object models; bounded preservation of classical syllogistic inference; a reusable Object Differential Kernel (ODK) and contract-governed Object Plugins; SQL-to-World-Entity (WE) adaptation through Bucketize, Normalize, Combine, Encode, and Derive; and WE-native Constrained Delta Evolution (CDE) for individual and composite states. The framework also explores CDE as an evolutionary-localization complement within the Structural Intelligence Framework (SIF), multi-agent capability relay and collective learning, potential structural capability spillover, and governance of AI-generated code through Calling Graph and Universal Token Node (UTN) interfaces. The v1.0.0 repository assembles conceptual papers, proposed specifications and contracts, domain cases, extensions, architecture figures, explicit unresolved questions, falsifiable hypotheses, and a staged validation roadmap. It follows the principles of Specifications/Contracts First, Conformance Before Runtime, and Explicit Leftovers. These are proposed research directions, not claims of a validated implementation, demonstrated cross-domain scaling, or established empirical results; the eleven core hypotheses remain at the proposed (E0) evidence stage.
